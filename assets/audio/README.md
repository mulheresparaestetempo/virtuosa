# Audio Files

Audio content for devotionals, prayers, and music.

## Organization

- **devotionals/** - Devotional audio recordings
- **prayers/** - Prayer audio recordings
- **music/** - Background music and hymns

## Format

- MP3 format (128kbps minimum)
- WAV for source files
- Mono or Stereo as appropriate

## Metadata

Include ID3 tags:
- Title
- Artist/Creator
- Duration
- Year

## Copyright

All audio must have proper licensing and attribution.
